HOW TO PLAY

✂️ MODE 1 — TAIL REMOVAL (CLASSIC / GATE / SURVIVAL / SAFE)

Shrink your tail to just 1 segment before the bot does! Collect red food to shrink (−2 length) and avoid green food which grows you (+4 length).

  • Both players can eat any food! Compete for the red food and force the opponent to eat green!
  • First to reach length 1 wins!
  • Safe Chambers: Survive the Energy Storm every 15s. Find a glowing cyan chamber outside the arena or rapidly gain length while exposed! Only one snake per chamber.
  • Gate Arena: Expanding chambers open after 25s, locking for 12s on entry.
  • Survival Chambers: Every 15s, one random gate opens for 7s. First to enter gets safety in the outside chamber. If both snakes stay inside, TWO futuristic cannons emerge to blast them for 10s (+2 length per hit).

    • ⚔️ MODE 2 — PREDATOR

    Fight inside a circular arena. Play a Best-of-3 Round System.

  • Hit opponent's body with your head to deal damage (−1 HP)
  • The arena alternates between Normal (10s) and Danger Phase (15s). During Danger Phase, the boundary glows red and damages any snake that touches it.
  • You cannot collide head-to-head — use your body as a weapon

    • 🌀 MODE 3 — SHRINK TO WIN

    Race in a larger arena! Camera follows your snake like Slither.io. Collect shrink food to reduce your length. First to reach length 1 wins (Bot mode) or aim for fastest time (Self mode).

  • Gravity Zones 🌀 teleport you instantly — avoid them!
  • Black Holes 🕳️ directly trap you in an orbit and prevent you from eating — wait them out!
  • Enemy Hunters 👹 actively chase you down! Getting hit adds +10 to your length.
  • Slower 🐌 slows your snake down for 5 seconds.
  • Rival snakes compete for the same food — be quick!
  • Slipo ✨ temporarily coats serpents in silver, making food run away if approached from the front!
  • Hurdles appear randomly for a brief duration followed by a wait.
  • Pulito Black Anomaly 🌪️ behaves like a living black hole, pulling all food toward it — survive the void!
