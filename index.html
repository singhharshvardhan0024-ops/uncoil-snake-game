HOW TO PLAY

✂️ MODE 1 — CLASSIC TAIL REMOVAL

Shrink your tail to just 1 segment before the bot does! Collect red food to shrink (−4 length) and avoid green food which grows you (+5 length).

  • Both players can eat any food! Compete for the red food and force the opponent to eat green!
  • First to reach length 1 wins!

    • 🚪 MODE 2 — GATE ARENA

    Tail Removal rules apply, but with expanding chambers!

  • Gates open after 25 seconds allowing access to 4 chambers.
  • Classic Red/Green food will continue to spawn in the main arena!
  • Entering a chamber starts a 12-second lockdown timer.
  • If caught in a chamber or gate when it closes, you are safely teleported to the center.

    • ⏳ MODE 3 — SURVIVAL CHAMBERS

    A survival-focused version of Tail Removal.

  • Gates open after 25 seconds. Enter top/bottom chambers holding RED food only!
  • Entering triggers a 12-second lockdown. Gates close and main-circle food vanishes.
  • If you are caught crossing a gate when it closes, you are forced completely into the chamber.
  • Survive inside the locked chamber with static food until gates reopen!

    • ⚔️ MODE 4 — PREDATOR

    Fight inside a circular arena. Play a Best-of-3 Round System.

  • Hit opponent's body with your head to deal damage (−1 HP) / length decrease
  • Hitting the arena wall slows you briefly
  • You cannot collide head-to-head — use your body as a weapon

    • 🌀 MODE 5 — SHRINK TO WIN

    Race in a larger arena! Camera follows your snake like Slither.io. Collect shrink food to reduce your length. First to reach length 1 wins (Bot mode) or aim for fastest time (Self mode).

  • Gravity Zones 🌀 teleport you instantly — avoid them!
  • Black Holes 🕳️ directly trap you in an orbit and prevent you from eating — wait them out!
  • Enemy Hunters 👹 actively chase you down! Getting hit adds +10 to your length.
  • Slower 🐌 slows your snake down for 5 seconds.
  • Rival snakes compete for the same food — be quick!
  • Hurdles alternate, appearing for a brief duration followed by a wait
  • Pulito Sandstorm 🌪️ pulls your serpent towards its center and deviates your path — try to escape!
