HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

UNCOIL is a reverse snake battle game. Choose speed or power and fight inside a circular arena. Reduce your opponent's 15 HP to zero to win.

COMBAT

  • Hit opponent's body with your head to deal damage (−1 HP)
  • Hitting the arena wall slows you briefly
  • You cannot collide head-to-head — use your body as a weapon

    • SNAKES

  • ⚡ SWIFT — Speed 10, Attack 4, Defense 3. Fast turns, easy to dodge
  • 💥 TITAN — Speed 4, Attack 8, Defense 10. Slow but devastating hits
  • Matches must be SWIFT vs TITAN — mirror matches not allowed

    • CONTROLS

  • Player 1 (SWIFT): WASD keys to steer
  • Player 2 (TITAN / local): Arrow keys to steer
  • Mobile: On-screen virtual joysticks

    • BOT MODES

  • Easy — slow reactions, mostly random movement
  • Medium — balanced pursuit and defense
  • Hard — predictive attacks, aggressive dodging
