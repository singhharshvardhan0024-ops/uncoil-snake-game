HOW TO PLAY

✂️ MODE 1 — TAIL REMOVAL

Shrink your tail to just 1 segment before the bot does! Collect red food to shrink (−2 length) and avoid green food which grows you (+3 length).

  • Food is player-specific: you can only eat your own food and will pass right through your opponent's
  • Food spawns ahead of your snake — you have 2 seconds to react
  • After 2 seconds food disappears and new food spawns
  • First to reach length 1 wins!

    • ⚔️ MODE 2 — PREDATOR

    Fight inside a circular arena. Reduce your opponent's 15 HP to zero to win.

  • Hit opponent's body with your head to deal damage (−1 HP)
  • Hitting the arena wall slows you briefly
  • You cannot collide head-to-head — use your body as a weapon

    • 🌀 MODE 3 — SHRINK TO WIN

    Race in a larger arena! Camera follows your snake like Slither.io. Collect shrink food to reduce your length. First to reach length 1 wins (Bot mode) or aim for fastest time (Self mode).

  • Gravity Zones 🌀 teleport you instantly — avoid them!
  • Black Holes 🕳️ directly trap you in an orbit and prevent you from eating — wait them out!
  • Rival snakes compete for the same food — be quick!
  • Hurdles alternate, appearing for a brief duration followed by a wait
  • Use the minimap (bottom-right) to track the full arena

    • CONTROLS

  • Player 1: A / D keys to steer
  • Player 2 (local Predator): Arrow Left / Right to steer
  • Mobile: On-screen virtual joystick

    • BOT DIFFICULTY

  • Easy — slow, random, makes mistakes often
  • Medium — moderate tracking, feints, prediction
  • Hard — predictive targeting, traps, counter-attacks
