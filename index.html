HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

UNCOIL is a serpent battle game. Choose your snake and fight inside a circular arena. Reduce your opponent's 15 HP to zero to win.

COMBAT

  • Hit opponent's body with your head to deal damage (−1 HP)
  • Hitting the arena wall slows you briefly
  • You cannot collide head-to-head — use your body as a weapon

    • SNAKES

  • ⚡ SPECTER — Balanced combatant. Speed 7, Attack 7, Defense 7.
  • 💥 LEVIATHAN — Balanced combatant. Speed 7, Attack 7, Defense 7.
  • Matches must be SPECTER vs LEVIATHAN — mirror matches not allowed

    • CONTROLS

  • Player 1 (SPECTER): A / D keys to steer
  • Player 2 (LEVIATHAN / local): Arrow Left / Right to steer
  • Mobile: On-screen virtual joysticks

    • BOT DIFFICULTY

  • Easy — slow reactions, random movement, misses often, drifts into walls
  • Medium — moderate tracking, feints, short prediction, wall avoidance
  • Hard — predictive targeting, traps, baiting retreats, counter-attacks
